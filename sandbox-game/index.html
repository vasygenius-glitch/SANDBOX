Spawn Menu

Controls: WASD to move. Space to jump. Shift to sprint. Ctrl to crouch.

Hold 'Q' to open menu.

Tools: Press 1: PhysGun, 2: Weld Tool, 3: Thruster Tool.

PhysGun: Left Click to grab. Scroll to move closer/further. Hold 'E' and use Arrow keys to rotate.

Thruster: Left Click to place. Hold 'T' or 'Numpad8' to activate.

Weld: Click two objects to weld them together.
